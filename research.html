Research Interests

Geometric Control Theory

Controllability on the group of diffeomorphisms, applications to fluid dynamics. Geometric methods in optimal control.

Control of PDEs

Bilinear Schrödinger equation: controllability, applications, numerics.

Multi-agent systems

Opinion dynamics and alignement systems: modelisation, sparse stabilization, controllability, and optimal control.
